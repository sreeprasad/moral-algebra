# Decide Scientifically

**Stop overthinking. Start weighing.**

<img width="1974" height="1686" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-08 at 10 17 16" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b863a994-fff5-40f1-a702-a790a4f2a300" />


You already know how to make a hard decision: draw a line down a page, write the pros on one side and the cons on the other, and stare at it.

This is that page. It just does the math.

**Write it down.** Your question, your option, every reason for and against, in your own handwriting on a ruled legal pad.

**Weigh it.** Every reason gets 1 to 5 dots. "Higher pay" is not the same as "nicer coffee." The page keeps a running tally: pros, cons, net.

**Compare two options.** Job A or job B. Rent or buy. Ship the side project or hike Mission Peak. Two columns, side by side, one winner.

**Perfect tie?** It counts down 3, 2, 1 and flips a coin. Science.

**Still not sure?** Hit *Decide for me*. An AI model reads every reason, re-weighs what really matters, and stamps a verdict on the page, along with the reasons that tipped the scale.

**Share it.** Every decision lives in its own link. Send it to the friend who keeps asking what you're going to do.

Benjamin Franklin called this method *moral algebra*. He did it with a quill.
